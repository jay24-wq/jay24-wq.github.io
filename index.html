2026August 2026Research paper · under review#AI

Research paper · submitted to JNATIA

I helped build a skin-lesion classifier that doesn't cheat and can show you where it's looking.

Most HAM10000 papers leak the same lesion into train and test, then call the CNN a black box. Ours does neither.

  • 82%accuracy, leakage-free test set
  • 0.73macro F1-score

// under the hood

Seven-class MobileNetV2 classifier with transfer learning. Lesion-based Stratified Group K-Fold splitting to eliminate data leakage (0 shared lesion IDs across train, validation and test), WeightedRandomSampler with on-the-fly augmentation against a 58:1 class imbalance, macro-F1-based early stopping to keep performance balanced across classes, and Grad-CAM to verify predictions rely on clinically relevant lesion regions.

  • PyTorch
  • MobileNetV2
  • Grad-CAM
  • Transfer learning
  • Stratified Group K-Fold
  • WeightedRandomSampler
